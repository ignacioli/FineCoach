# FineCoach Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-20

FineCoach (精练教练) is an iPhone app for personal trainers to record training sessions. This policy explains how the app handles your data. The app's interface is in Simplified Chinese; this page is an English version of the same policy.

## Summary

FineCoach has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your own iPhone. The developer cannot receive or see any of your data.

## What we collect

Nothing.

- The app does not require registration or sign-in.
- The app does not send data to the developer or to any third party over the network. The code makes no network requests.
- The app contains no third-party SDKs: no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
- The app does not use HealthKit and does not read system data such as contacts, photos or location.

## Where the data you enter is stored

The student profiles, training records, body data, injury notes, schedules, course packages and notes you enter are stored only in the app's own storage on this iPhone:

- The files use iOS file protection, so they are encrypted by the system while the device is locked.
- The files are excluded from iCloud and from computer-based device backups.
- Deleting the app deletes this data with it. If you lose your phone, the data is lost too, unless you made a backup yourself beforehand (see below).

## When data leaves this phone

Data leaves this phone only when you choose to do so, always through the iOS share sheet or "Save to Files". FineCoach does not host files and does not create public links.

1. **Backup** (Settings → Backup & Restore): creates an `.fcbackup` file encrypted with the password you set. You decide where the file goes.
2. **Personal training archive** (student detail → Export personal training archive): creates an `.fcarchive` file encrypted with the password you set, so the student can view it read-only on their own device. Injuries and precautions, coach notes, private exercise notes, course packages, schedules, session adjustments, phone and WeChat contacts, exercise images and training templates are never exported.
3. **Training report**: creates an image whose contents you choose. Gender, body data, injuries, phone, WeChat and your private notes never appear in a report.

Once a file has been handed off, the app or service you choose (for example WeChat or the Files app) handles it under its own policy. After a file has been received, neither you nor FineCoach can take it back.

## Notifications

The app asks for notification permission only when you turn on a reminder for a class. Notifications are generated locally on your iPhone and do not go through any server.

## External links

The "Source code" and license links open the corresponding web pages in the system browser. After that, the website's own data practices apply.

## Student view

If you choose "Import a personal training archive" at first launch, the app stores only the one archive you import, for read-only viewing on this device. An imported archive also stays only on this device and is never uploaded.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes in the future (for example, if networking is added), this policy will be updated first, before the corresponding version is released.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue on GitHub: <https://github.com/ignacioli/FineCoach/issues>.
